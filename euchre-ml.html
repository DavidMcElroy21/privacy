Euchre ML — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Euchre ML ("the app") is a card game published by DUST DATA AND AI, LLC.

The short version

The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no accounts. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

Game settings, achievement progress, trial-game counts, and purchase state are stored locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app does NOT do

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • The app makes no network connections during play.
  • No data is shared with the developer or any third party.

Purchases

Paid difficulty ranks are one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The developer receives Google Play's standard sales reporting but never sees your payment details.

Children

The app is a standard card game with no chat, no user content, no ads, and no data collection.
